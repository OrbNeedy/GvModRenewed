The Azure Striker Mod allows you to utilize the power of the seventh life wave.

It adds a new class, the septima class, which doesn't rely on weapons, instead mainly using the player's seventh life wave, or more commonly known as septima.
The content added includes:
* Two separate septimas: Azure Striker and Rebirth, with different skills and play styles
* 37 accessories
* 7 armor sets
* A skill system based on ASG1 and ASG2's system, with 23 different special skills, ranging from buffs to attacks

To start, select a septima in the start menu, then try to craft a weapon to tag enemies, as your septima is otherwise very weak.
Once you start exploring the world, you may find strange capsules on the ground around the world and in chests, you can use these to upgrade your septima and not only make it stronger, but also unlock special skills to help you along the way.
From this point you can create accessories and armor with the new materials found from beating enemies.
